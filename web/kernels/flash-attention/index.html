Every rate counts the same 4 × 32 × 4,096² × 128 = 274.9 billion operations. The unit rails are the book's idealised cycle budget (§9.5, §10.3): two products take 8,388,608 operations per 128 × 128 tile, and the tile needs 16,384 exponentials at 16 per SM per cycle. The "rest of the softmax" blocks are drawn at 10% of the exponentials' length. The book did not measure them, so their length is a schematic. The tile view's byte counts are global-memory requests by one block at N = 1,024; the L2 cache may serve re-reads of K and V.
